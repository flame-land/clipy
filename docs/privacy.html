Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy describes information handled when you use Clipy, a Discord bot for YouTube clip search and processing supported public media links. It describes the bot's intended current behavior; Discord and third-party websites have their own separate data practices.

1. Information Clipy processes

  • Command and interaction data: Discord provides information needed to handle a command, such as your Discord user ID, interaction or message context, server context, and the command options you submit.
  • Search terms and URLs: search queries are sent to YouTube search through yt-dlp; submitted download URLs are passed to yt-dlp to retrieve public media from the supported platform.
  • Operational error details: the bot may log a source hostname and an error type to help diagnose failed downloads. It is not intended to log your Discord token or full submitted URL.

2. Files and retention

Downloaded media is written to a temporary working directory so the bot can send it as a Discord attachment. The intended behavior is to remove these temporary files after processing. This does not control copies held by Discord, the original platform, hosting providers, or recipients who save an attachment. Temporary files may remain if the process crashes or the host fails unexpectedly.

3. Sharing with third parties

Clipy uses Discord to receive commands and send replies, and contacts the relevant third-party media platform to search or retrieve a URL you submit. Those services may receive technical information such as IP address, request details, cookies or other data handled by their own systems. Review their privacy policies for details. The bot operator does not sell your personal information.

4. Storage and analytics

The current code is not designed to maintain a user profile database or analytics history. Hosting providers may independently keep infrastructure logs. This statement should be revisited if the bot adds databases, analytics, external APIs, or new logging.

5. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to operate the bot, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send passwords, Discord tokens, or other secrets to Clipy. The bot operator should keep credentials private and restrict access to the hosting environment.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the bot at any time and avoid submitting search terms or URLs. To ask a question or raise a privacy concern, contact the operator through the Clipy GitHub repository. Please do not post personal information or secrets publicly.

7. Children and legal requests

Do not use Clipy in a way that violates applicable age requirements or laws. The operator may disclose information if required by applicable law; any information available to the operator is limited by what the bot and hosting environment actually retain.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's behavior or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published here.
